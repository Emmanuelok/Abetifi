# Media provenance

This register covers the synthetic visual assets used in the Abetifi Stone Age Community Development website. It is part of the release record and must remain with the source.

## Public disclosure standard

None of the generated stills is documentary photography, archaeological evidence, an approved architectural rendering, proof of current visitor access, or evidence of a committed partnership. Every still is accompanied in the interface by a visible “Original interpretive visualisation” or “Concept visualisation” caption.

The existing landing film is also synthetic and carries this disclosure:

> AI-generated interpretive visualisation inspired by Ghana’s Kwahu Plateau; not documentary footage or an archaeological reconstruction of Bosumpra Cave.

## Generation and art direction

- Generator: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`.
- Model: not exposed by the generation tool and therefore not inferred.
- Generation date: 2026-08-06.
- Reference images: none.
- Rights class: original generated media.
- Shared visual direction: laterite, quartz, deep forest, restrained copper, natural Ghanaian plateau light, believable contemporary materials and human scale.
- Exclusions: generated readable text, logos, named institutions, documentary claims, ceremonial-costume shorthand, charity framing, “primitive life” clichés, treasure imagery, fantasy caves, money graphics, handshakes and fake charts.

The generated people are fictional composites. They must not be identified as Abetifi residents, project staff, researchers, investors, guides or endorsers.

## Source-photography review

Wikimedia Commons was reviewed for openly licensed Abetifi and Kwahu context. No reusable documentary photograph of Bosumpra Rockshelter itself was identified. Broader Kwahu photographs were not substituted for Bosumpra, and publisher-controlled photographs from the supplied archaeology papers were not republished.

## Delivery checks

- All public stills are WebP and decode successfully.
- Landing stills are 1,536–1,672 pixels wide and 88–204 KB.
- Interior-route stills are 1,672 × 941 pixels and 84–320 KB.
- All stills have purpose-written alt text and visible disclosure captions.
- The website retains a reduced-motion path and does not rely on image content alone for factual claims.
